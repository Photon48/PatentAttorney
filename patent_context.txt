Abstract Text:
This invention presents a novel water purification system that markedly elevates potability standards through the integration of nanocomposite filtration and UV-C LED photocatalytic technology. Designed to efficiently remove a broad spectrum of contaminants including microorganisms, heavy metals, and organic compounds, this system ensures unparalleled water safety and quality.

Central to the system is a cutting-edge nanocomposite filter, comprising nano-sized materials each targeting specific contaminants. The multi-layered filter features a magnetic nanolayer for heavy metals, a carbon-based nanolayer for organic compounds, and a bioactive nanolayer for microbial deactivation. This filtration stage is followed by a UV-C LED disinfection process, utilizing energy-efficient LEDs that emit germicidal ultraviolet light, ensuring complete deactivation of any remaining microorganisms and degradation of organic pollutants.

A key innovation of this system is the incorporation of a smart monitoring system. Equipped with real-time sensors for filter and UV-C LED performance, it employs algorithms to predict maintenance needs, ensuring optimal operation and consistent water quality. Users benefit from a user-friendly interface providing updates on system status and water quality.

The system's design emphasizes versatility and scalability, suitable for applications ranging from home use to industrial water treatment. It represents a leap forward in water purification technology, combining nanotechnology, photonic disinfection, and intelligent system management to offer a sustainable, effective solution for global water safety challenges. This invention aims to set a new standard in water purification, addressing the critical need for clean drinking water with an innovative and comprehensive approach.
